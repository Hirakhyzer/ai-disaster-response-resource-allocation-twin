# Disaster response report template

## Scenario
Describe the synthetic disaster type, severity, and planning horizon.

## Demand
Summarize highest-demand zones and forecast drivers.

## Capacity
List high-pressure shelters, hospitals, hubs, and staging areas.

## Logistics
Summarize high-delay routes and access-fragile zones.

## Allocation
Show resource coverage ratios and unmet units.

## Equity
Highlight vulnerable zones with lower service ratios.

## Planning boundary
State that outputs are synthetic planning-support signals, not operational orders.
